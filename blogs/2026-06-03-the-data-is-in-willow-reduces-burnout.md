We don’t need a clinical study to tell us that supporting moms helps them thrive, but that’s exactly what we got from the JAMA Network. A recent clinical study published in the JAMA Network found that postpartum physician trainees who received a structured parental support package — including a Willow pump — reported significantly less burnout than those who didn't. We know, all of us moms are thinking: YEAH, OF COURSE.
