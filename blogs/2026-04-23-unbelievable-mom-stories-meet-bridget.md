Read the stories real-life moms are sharing about their experience What’s something you once thought was “just part of being a mom” that you now realize is actually unbelievable? I used to think the process of pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum was “just part of being a mom.” As the youngest child, I never saw my mom go through this, but growing up I did see my aunts and other women experiencing everything from pregnancy to postpartum all while working. I never realized what they were going through until I went through it myself.
