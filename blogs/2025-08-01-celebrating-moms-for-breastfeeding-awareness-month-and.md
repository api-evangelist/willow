Willow is on a mission to elevate the maternal experience, whether or not there’s a pump involved. Breastfeeding may be natural, but that doesn’t mean it always comes naturally. In fact, while 83% of mothers start out breastfeeding , a staggering 70% of mothers experience breastfeeding difficulties in the first month, often resulting in poorer breastfeeding outcomes, as well as a significant physical, emotional, and mental impact on moms.
