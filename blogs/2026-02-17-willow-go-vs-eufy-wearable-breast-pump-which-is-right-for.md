Introduction While wearable breast pumps have become popular, not every company entering this space understands maternal anatomy. Willow launched the world's first all-in-one in-bra wearable pump in 2017 and is a maternal health company built specifically for pumping and breastfeeding mothers. Eufy is a smart home brand known for security cameras and robot vacuums – the company just recently extended into breast pumps as a product line addition.
